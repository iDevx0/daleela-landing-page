Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How does Daleela work?

You tell us your symptoms and health history. One general doctor owns your case and consults our specialists across primary care, gynecology, dermatology, nutrition, endocrinology, and more. Together, they treat the connected picture, not one symptom at a time, and you get one clear treatment plan.

Do I need to know which specialty I need?

Not at all. Your general doctor owns your case and consults the right specialists. Explain your symptoms, and they decide what you need.

How is this different from seeing several doctors?

When you see several doctors, each one treats a single symptom, and you retell your story from the start every time. At Daleela, one general doctor owns your case and consults specialists across primary care, gynecology, dermatology, nutrition, endocrinology, and more, so they treat the connected picture with one treatment plan.

Which doctors will follow my case?

One general doctor owns your case and consults licensed specialists across primary care, gynecology, dermatology, nutrition, endocrinology, and more. You can meet the team in the “Your medical team” section above.

Will I need lab tests?

If your case needs them, the team will request only the necessary tests, to confirm the diagnosis. You won’t do tests you don’t need.

Can I send photos of my symptoms?

Yes. You can add photos in the chat while you tell us about your case, like skin or hair photos, so the team sees the full picture.

Is what I share private and safe?

Yes. Your privacy and the safety of your information are a priority. You can talk about anything, including sensitive topics like sexual health, safely and without judgment.

What if I notice a new symptom after treatment starts?

Your general doctor stays with you for any question or new symptom throughout your treatment.

Is Daleela for emergencies?

No. Daleela is for follow-up and treatment, not emergencies. If you have heavy bleeding, sudden severe pain, or any serious symptom, go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

Download daleela app from the button below, start the chat, and explain your symptoms.
