What can your medical team help with?

Irregular periods Late, missing, or irregular? We look for the cause, not a temporary fix.
Acne and skin Breakouts that come back with every cycle? The cause is often hormonal, and we treat it at the root.
Weight management and PCOS A plan that brings together nutrition, hormones, and medication if needed, including an assessment of weight-loss injections.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start.

How does Daleela work?

You tell us your symptoms and health history in a short chat. A team of specialist doctors reviews your case together and connects every symptom. Then you get one clear treatment plan, and one doctor responsible for your care follows up with you until you feel better.

Do I need to know which specialty I need?

Not at all. You don’t have to pick a specialty or know who to see. Tell us how you feel, and the team decides what you need.

How is this different from seeing several doctors?

When you see several doctors, each one treats a single symptom, and you retell your story from the start every time. At Daleela, gynecology, dermatology, endocrinology, and nutrition doctors see your case together, so connected issues are treated with one plan.

Which doctors will follow my case?

Licensed doctors from different specialties: obstetrics and gynecology, dermatology, endocrinology, and nutrition. You can meet them in the “Your medical team” section above.

Will I need lab tests?

If your case needs them, the team will request only the necessary tests, to confirm the diagnosis. You won’t do tests you don’t need.

Can I send photos of my symptoms?

Yes. You can add photos in the chat while you tell us about your case, like skin or hair photos, so the team sees the full picture.

Is what I share private and safe?

Yes. Your privacy and the safety of your information are a priority. You can talk about anything, including sensitive topics like sexual health, safely and without judgment.

What if I notice a new symptom after treatment starts?

Your doctor stays with you for any question or new symptom throughout your treatment, until you feel better.

Is Daleela for emergencies?

No. Daleela is for follow-up and treatment, not emergencies. If you have heavy bleeding, sudden severe pain, or any serious symptom, go to the nearest emergency room immediately.
